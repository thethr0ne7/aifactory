# Marketing Psychology

## Purpose

Apply behavioral science and decision heuristics to messaging, offers, UX and campaigns without manipulative dark patterns.

## Workflow

1. Define target behavior and user context.
2. Diagnose friction, uncertainty, motivation and social proof needs.
3. Select relevant principles only when they fit observed behavior.
4. Convert principle into testable design/message changes.
5. Identify ethical risk and potential unintended effects.
6. Recommend experiment and measurement plan.

## Guardrails

Psychological principles are hypotheses, not guarantees. Avoid deceptive scarcity, coercion and hidden consent.
