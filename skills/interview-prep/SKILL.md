# Interview Prep

## Purpose

Prepare structured candidate or interviewer materials: role competencies, questions, STAR evidence, scorecards and practice loops.

## Workflow

1. Identify role, seniority, interview type and evaluation criteria.
2. Map competencies and likely question families.
3. Build evidence-backed STAR stories or interviewer scorecard prompts.
4. Run practice questions and critique specificity, ownership and outcomes.
5. Identify gaps and create targeted preparation plan.
6. Keep hiring evaluation job-related and consistent across candidates.

## Guardrails

Do not infer protected traits or recommend discriminatory screening.
