# SEO Audit

## Purpose

Audit technical, on-page and structural SEO and convert findings into a prioritized repair plan.

## Workflow

1. Define site scope, target markets and business goals.
2. Check crawl/indexability, canonicalization, robots/sitemaps and status behavior.
3. Review information architecture, internal linking, metadata, headings and content intent.
4. Separate observed defects from hypotheses requiring Search Console/log data.
5. Prioritize by impact, confidence and effort.
6. Produce issue → evidence → consequence → fix → verification table.

## Guardrails

Do not promise rankings or traffic gains from static inspection.
