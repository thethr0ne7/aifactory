# Pricing

## Purpose

Design or audit pricing, packaging and monetization using customer value, willingness-to-pay evidence, cost structure and strategic positioning.

## Workflow

1. Define buyer, unit of value and purchase motion.
2. Map current alternatives and competitor pricing.
3. Separate price metric, package boundaries, discounting and billing cadence.
4. Test value-based, cost-based and competitive anchors.
5. Model revenue, margin, conversion and expansion implications under scenarios.
6. Identify price sensitivity assumptions requiring validation.
7. Recommend experiment or rollout with guardrails.

## Guardrails

Do not claim willingness-to-pay without evidence. Preserve uncertainty in scenario outputs.
