# Compensation Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze salary, variable compensation, equity and band placement using role level, market data, internal equity and budget constraints.

## Workflow

1. Define geography, role family, level, employment type and compensation components.
2. Validate data vintage and benchmark comparability.
3. Calculate range position, compa-ratio and total compensation scenarios where inputs allow.
4. Compare external competitiveness and internal equity separately.
5. Test budget impact of adjustments.
6. Flag statistical/sample limitations and policy/legal constraints.
7. Produce options and decision rationale.

## Guardrails

Do not infer compensation from protected traits. Market benchmarks are volatile and require current sourcing.
