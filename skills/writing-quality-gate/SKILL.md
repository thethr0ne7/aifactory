# Writing Quality Gate

## Purpose

Remove formulaic AI prose, filler and low-trust writing after evidence/domain validation without damaging verified wording.

## Activate when

A user-facing report, memo, research brief, product copy or explanatory document is ready for publication.

## Placement

`DRAFT → EVIDENCE VALIDATION → DOMAIN VALIDATION → WRITING QUALITY → PUBLISH`

## Checks

- directness;
- rhythm and sentence variation;
- trust/precision;
- authenticity/naturalness;
- density/readability;
- filler/throat-clearing;
- vague declaratives;
- repetitive binary contrasts;
- passive or bureaucratic phrasing where unnecessary;
- excessive paragraph density.

## Protected spans

Never stylistically rewrite verified quotations, legal names, source titles, identifiers, numerical values or other evidence-locked spans unless correction is explicitly required and reverified.

## Decision

Use a clear pass/revise threshold appropriate to the artifact; the factory's historic default benchmark is 35/50 across five quality dimensions.

## Guardrails

Evidence fidelity outranks style. Do not make language more confident than the underlying evidence.
