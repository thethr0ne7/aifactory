# Research & Truth

## Purpose

Produce evidence-grounded research for high-cost decisions where source quality, uncertainty and traceability matter.

## Activate when

The task concerns law, grants, public funding, policy, regulations, financial models, technical audits, scientific/medical claims, eligibility, contracts or any decision where a plausible wrong answer is expensive.

## Workflow

`QUALIFY → SOURCE STRATEGY → RETRIEVE → EXTRACT → EVIDENCE CHECK → CONTRADICTION SCAN → RISK MAP → DRAFT → REVIEW → TRACEBACK`

1. Define the exact question, jurisdiction/time horizon and decision consequence.
2. Prefer primary/official sources; use secondary sources for discovery or independent interpretation.
3. Preserve source title, owner, date/version, URL/identifier and exact relevant passage where appropriate.
4. Classify each important claim as `CONFIRMED`, `OBSERVED`, `ASSUMPTION`, `UNKNOWN` or `BLOCKER`.
5. Separate facts from forecasts and recommendations.
6. Search for evidence that could falsify the leading conclusion.
7. Explain material contradictions rather than averaging them away.
8. Produce consequences, decision options and next evidence/action.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order when applicable:

1. controlling law/regulation/official register or first-party technical documentation;
2. official guidance, datasets, budget documents or owner publications;
3. peer-reviewed/primary research;
4. reputable independent analysis;
5. discovery-only sources.

## Guardrails

- A search-result snippet is not sufficient evidence for a consequential claim.
- A future intention is not an adopted rule.
- A trend is not an eligibility criterion.
- A machine match is not human verification.
- Missing evidence must remain visible.

## Output contract

Return a concise decision brief with: question, verified findings, contradictions, uncertainty, risks, likely consequences, recommendation/decision options, and traceable sources.
