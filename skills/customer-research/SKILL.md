# Customer Research

## Purpose

Turn interviews, reviews, support logs, surveys and behavioral evidence into customer problems, language, segments and decision-relevant insights.

## Workflow

1. Define research question and target segment.
2. Preserve raw evidence separately from interpretation.
3. Extract jobs, pains, triggers, objections, alternatives and desired outcomes.
4. Cluster recurring themes and tag evidence strength/frequency.
5. Separate stated preference from demonstrated behavior.
6. Identify contradictions and missing segments.
7. Produce insight table, verbatim-safe themes, hypotheses and next research questions.

## Guardrails

Never invent customer quotes, sample sizes or prevalence.
