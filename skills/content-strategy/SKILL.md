# Content Strategy

## Purpose

Build a content system tied to ICP, funnel jobs, search/distribution opportunities and measurable business outcomes.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, positioning and business objective.
2. Map customer questions by awareness/funnel stage.
3. Build themes, formats, channels and distribution logic.
4. Balance evergreen, demand-capture and demand-creation content.
5. Define editorial cadence, ownership and reuse paths.
6. Attach metrics to each content job rather than vanity output volume.
