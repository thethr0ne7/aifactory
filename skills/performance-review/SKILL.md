# Performance Review

## Purpose

Create fair, evidence-based self reviews, manager reviews, peer feedback and calibration preparation.

## Workflow

1. Define subject, review period, role expectations and review type.
2. Gather observable outcomes, behaviors, feedback and metrics.
3. Separate evidence from interpretation.
4. Structure strengths and development areas with specific examples.
5. Tie assessment to agreed expectations rather than personality.
6. Flag missing evidence and potential recency/bias effects.
7. Produce draft for human review, never auto-submit.

## Guardrails

Avoid protected-trait inference, medical speculation and unsupported performance claims.
