# SEO / GEO

## Purpose

Improve discoverability in traditional search and AI-generated answers without collapsing technical SEO, content quality and authority into one vague score.

## Activate when

The task concerns crawlability, indexing, search performance, structured data, content search intent, local relevance, entity clarity or generative-engine/AI discovery.

## Workflow

`CRAWL/ACCESS → TECHNICAL → CONTENT → SCHEMA → INDEXABILITY → AUTHORITY/RELEVANCE → GEO/AI DISCOVERY → PRIORITY → VERIFY`

1. Confirm target pages, market/language and business goal.
2. Check crawl/access/indexability fundamentals.
3. Inspect metadata, canonicalization, internal links, page structure and performance signals.
4. Evaluate content against real user intent and evidence, not keyword stuffing.
5. Validate structured data against actual page content.
6. Improve entity clarity, source authority and answerable structure for AI discovery where relevant.
7. Prioritize fixes by impact and confidence.
8. Recheck after implementation.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise rankings.
- GEO is not a license to mass-produce synthetic content.
- Structured data must not claim facts absent from the page.
- Separate technical blockers from editorial opportunities.
