# Competitive Platform Analysis

## Purpose

Compare competing platforms across product, positioning, pricing, distribution, ecosystem, switching cost and strategic moat.

## Workflow

1. Define direct competitors, substitutes and adjacent platforms.
2. Compare target users, core jobs, feature architecture, integrations, pricing and go-to-market motion.
3. Capture evidence from product pages, docs, pricing, changelogs, reviews and public customer signals.
4. Build capability matrix without treating feature count as product quality.
5. Identify differentiation, parity traps, ecosystem leverage and switching friction.
6. Produce attack/defend opportunities and evidence gaps.

## Guardrails

Do not infer hidden revenue, usage or roadmap as fact.
