# Cold Email

## Purpose

Create concise outbound emails and follow-up sequences tied to a credible prospect trigger and a clear next step.

## Workflow

1. Confirm ICP, recipient role, offer and objective.
2. Use one defensible personalization signal; never fake familiarity.
3. Lead with relevance/problem, not company biography.
4. Keep claim density low and CTA small.
5. Produce subject options, first email and bounded follow-up sequence.
6. Add assumptions and evidence needed before sending at scale.

## Guardrails

Respect applicable anti-spam/privacy rules and user-approved sending authority. Drafting does not imply permission to send.
