# Canvas Design

## Purpose

Create strong static visual artifacts—posters, one-pagers, infographics, covers, share cards and presentation visuals—with deliberate composition and minimal textual clutter.

## Activate when

The desired result is a static visual artifact rather than a full interactive UI.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, surface, size/aspect ratio and one primary message.
2. Load Brand DNA when available.
3. Establish composition, hierarchy and focal point before adding detail.
4. Use typography and imagery as structural elements, not decoration.
5. Keep information density appropriate for viewing distance/device.
6. Validate legibility, contrast, cropping and export dimensions.
7. Compare with brief/reference through Visual Fidelity.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn a poster into a document page full of text.
- Do not invent brand assets or factual content.
- Preserve safe margins and platform crop zones.
- Use licensed/authorized assets only.
