# Marketing Campaign

## Purpose

Plan integrated campaigns from objective and audience through message, channel mix, assets, launch, measurement and postmortem.

## Workflow

1. Lock objective, audience, offer, budget, timing and constraints.
2. Define campaign thesis and success metrics.
3. Build channel/asset sequence and dependencies.
4. Specify tracking and attribution before launch.
5. Produce calendar, owners, creative briefs and QA checklist.
6. Launch only through authorized execution tools.
7. Measure results, separate signal from noise and capture learnings.
