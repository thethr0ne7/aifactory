# Brand & Theme Engine

## Purpose

Encode a product's own brand identity into reusable visual rules that stay consistent across UI, documents, slides, social assets and video.

## Activate when

A product has brand references, logo/colors/type rules, or needs a new coherent theme across multiple artifacts.

## Workflow

1. Collect authorized brand inputs and constraints.
2. Separate immutable identity from campaign/theme variation.
3. Define semantic color roles, typography roles, spacing, radii, iconography, imagery and tone.
4. Produce Brand DNA tokens plus do/don't rules.
5. Generate artifact-specific theme mappings without changing the core identity.
6. Validate contrast, legibility and cross-artifact consistency.

## Output contract

Return Brand DNA with:

- primary/secondary/accent/neutral semantic roles;
- typography scale and usage;
- component styling principles;
- imagery/illustration direction;
- motion personality when relevant;
- forbidden treatments;
- mappings for web/docs/slides/social/video.

## Guardrails

- Do not copy Anthropic or any upstream brand identity.
- Upstream brand-guideline skills are architectural references only.
- A theme may vary; core brand identity must not drift silently.

## Provenance

Adapted from structural ideas in `anthropics/skills` brand-guidelines and theme-factory.
