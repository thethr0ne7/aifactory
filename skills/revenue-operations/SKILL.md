# Revenue Operations

## Purpose

Design and diagnose the operating system connecting marketing, sales and customer success: lifecycle, routing, CRM hygiene, funnel definitions and revenue reporting.

## Workflow

1. Define lifecycle stages and ownership.
2. Audit source data, field definitions and handoff rules.
3. Map funnel conversion, velocity, leakage and SLA failures.
4. Separate reporting defects from actual performance defects.
5. Design routing, qualification, attribution and pipeline governance.
6. Produce operating metrics, repair backlog and owners.

## Guardrails

Do not treat CRM fields as ground truth without data-quality checks.
