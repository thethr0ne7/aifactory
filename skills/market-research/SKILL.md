# Market Research

## Purpose

Produce evidence-backed market maps, sizing ranges, trend signals, competitor sets and strategic implications for a real product or business.

## Activate when

The task asks about market size, market structure, category dynamics, demand, competitors, technology trends or entry attractiveness.

## Workflow

1. Lock market definition, geography, customer segment and time horizon.
2. Separate TAM/SAM/SOM from actually reachable demand.
3. Gather primary/current evidence before secondary summaries where possible.
4. Build competitor/category map and identify substitutes.
5. Quantify only where inputs are traceable; use ranges when precision is unsupported.
6. Run contradiction scan and identify unknowns.
7. Deliver market thesis, risks, opportunities and next validation actions.

## Guardrails

- Do not present top-down market numbers as directly obtainable revenue.
- Distinguish observed data, derived estimates and assumptions.
- Freshness matters for prices, regulation, funding and fast-moving technology.
