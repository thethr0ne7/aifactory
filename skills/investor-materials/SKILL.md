# Investor Materials

## Purpose

Build investor-ready pitch decks, one-pagers, memos and supporting financial narratives from verified company facts.

## Workflow

1. Lock audience, round/stage and funding objective.
2. Gather verified problem, solution, market, traction, business model, team, moat, use-of-funds and financial inputs.
3. Build coherent narrative: why now → why this problem → why this product → why this team → why this return profile.
4. Reconcile every metric across deck, model and memo.
5. Flag unsupported claims and assumptions explicitly.
6. Produce concise investor artifact plus diligence appendix/source ledger.

## Guardrails

Never fabricate traction, customers, market share, revenue, pipeline or financial projections.
