# Media / Video

## Purpose

Create repeatable programmatic video outputs for product demos, explainers, social clips, animated infographics and batch-rendered variants.

## Activate when

The deliverable is video and benefits from code-driven composition, repeatability, data-driven scenes or many variants.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, duration, aspect ratio, distribution surface and primary message.
2. Write scene beats before implementing animation.
3. Apply Brand DNA and Motion Director rules.
4. Separate content/data from reusable visual components.
5. Keep timing deterministic and composition safe for the target aspect ratio.
6. Validate text legibility, audio/subtitle behavior, frame bounds and render reliability.
7. Produce reusable parameterization when batch variants are expected.

## Guardrails

- Do not use programmatic video when a single simple edit is cheaper manually.
- Avoid text-heavy slides pretending to be video.
- Respect licensing for media, fonts and audio.
- Keep safe areas for mobile/social overlays.

## Provenance

Inspired by `remotion-dev/remotion` and its programmatic video model. Remotion is an implementation option, not an unconditional dependency.
