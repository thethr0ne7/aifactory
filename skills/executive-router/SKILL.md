# Executive Router

## Purpose

Turn ambiguous product or business work into a bounded decision frame by activating only the executive lenses that materially change the answer.

## Activate when

The task involves strategy, prioritization, product scope, economics, execution planning, architecture, positioning, acquisition, pricing or monetization.

## Lenses

- **CEO** — objective, priority, scope, opportunity cost, kill/ship.
- **CFO** — economics, budget, ROI, funding, unit economics, downside.
- **COO** — sequence, owners, dependencies, operating constraints, delivery risk.
- **CIO** — architecture, data, security, reliability, integration boundaries.
- **CMO** — audience, positioning, UX promise, brand, acquisition, communication.
- **CRO** — pricing, funnel, conversion, revenue model, sales path.

## Workflow

1. Restate the decision in one sentence.
2. Select only the lenses that could change the decision.
3. For each selected lens, identify evidence, constraints and failure conditions.
4. Resolve cross-lens conflicts explicitly; do not stage role-play dialogue.
5. Produce one integrated recommendation with `PASS`, `CONDITIONAL`, or `BLOCKED`.
6. Name the next decision or experiment.

## Output contract

Return:

- decision;
- activated lenses;
- decisive evidence/assumptions;
- key trade-offs;
- kill criteria;
- next action.

## Guardrails

- Never invoke all six lenses by default.
- Never create fictional debates or majority voting.
- A lens is a policy perspective, not an autonomous persona.
- If evidence is missing, route to Research & Truth rather than inventing confidence.
