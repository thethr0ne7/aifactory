# Copywriting

## Purpose

Write conversion-focused product and marketing copy grounded in audience, offer, proof and desired action.

## Workflow

1. Lock audience, page/channel, awareness level and CTA.
2. Gather product truth, differentiators, proof and objections.
3. Build message hierarchy before polishing language.
4. Prefer concrete outcomes and evidence over adjectives.
5. Produce headline/value proposition/body/CTA variants where useful.
6. Check claim support, clarity, specificity and consistency with brand voice.

## Guardrails

Never invent testimonials, customer counts, performance claims or guarantees.
