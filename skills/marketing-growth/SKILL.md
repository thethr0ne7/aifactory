# Marketing Growth

## Purpose

Route product-growth work to the smallest relevant marketing capability: positioning, copy, CRO, pricing, launch, acquisition or analytics.

## Activate when

The task asks how to attract, convert, retain or monetize users, or how to communicate product value.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, job-to-be-done and target business outcome.
2. Choose one or more relevant modes: positioning, copy, CRO, pricing, launch, acquisition, analytics.
3. Establish baseline/funnel evidence before proposing optimization.
4. Generate hypotheses tied to measurable outcomes.
5. Prioritize by expected impact, confidence, effort and reversibility.
6. Define experiment/success metric and stop condition.
7. Feed SEO/GEO tasks to the dedicated skill when search discovery is central.

## Guardrails

- Never load an entire marketing library by default.
- Do not fabricate customer research or conversion metrics.
- Avoid dark patterns or deceptive scarcity.
- Strategy must match actual product maturity and distribution constraints.
