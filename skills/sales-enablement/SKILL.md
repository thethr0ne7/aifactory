# Sales Enablement

## Purpose

Create evidence-consistent sales collateral: one-pagers, pitch narratives, objection handling, demo scripts, battlecards and proof assets.

## Workflow

1. Lock audience, sales stage and job-to-be-done.
2. Pull approved positioning, proof, pricing and competitive facts.
3. Build asset around buyer questions and decision criteria.
4. Separate claims, proof and objection responses.
5. Add discovery prompts and handoff instructions for reps.
6. Validate consistency with current product and pricing.
