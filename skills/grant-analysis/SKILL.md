# Grant Analysis

## Purpose

Evaluate grants, subsidies and public-support measures against a real applicant/project using traceable official rules.

## Activate when

The task asks whether a project qualifies, what funding route fits, what documents/actions are needed, or how a grant/support measure changes.

## Workflow

1. Identify jurisdiction, programme/measure, authority, current version and application window.
2. Recover official legal/competition documents and budget/implementation guidance.
3. Extract applicant, project, cost, co-financing, timing, exclusion and reporting requirements with provenance.
4. Separate mandatory eligibility from scoring/priority criteria.
5. Map verified project facts to requirements.
6. Mark each criterion: `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
7. Identify blockers, missing documents, financial obligations and deadlines.
8. Produce next decision/action and evidence pack.

## Guardrails

- A programme direction or press release is not an open grant call.
- Relevance is not eligibility.
- Candidate/unverified measures cannot support a definitive eligibility conclusion.
- Use current official sources for material claims.
