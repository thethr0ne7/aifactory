# Business, Sales, Marketing & HR Skill Pack

## Purpose

Domain router for the 20 requested specialist skills installed from the August 17, 2026 screenshot set.

## Routing

### Entrepreneurs
`market-research`, `customer-research`, `competitive-platform-analysis`, `pricing`, `investor-materials`

### Sales
`cold-email`, `sales-enablement`, `revenue-operations`, `prospecting`, `draft-outreach`

### Marketing
`seo-audit`, `copywriting`, `marketing-psychology`, `content-strategy`, `marketing-campaign`

### HR
`interview-prep`, `chro-advisor`, `performance-review`, `compensation-analysis`, `hr-coordinator`

Use the narrowest matching skill. Combine skills only when the requested deliverable genuinely spans domains.

## Shared rules

- Evidence-first for research, pricing, compensation and performance claims.
- No automatic outbound sending or HR record mutation without explicit authority.
- Current-source verification for volatile market, legal, pricing and compensation facts.
- Do not silently shrink user-requested scope.
