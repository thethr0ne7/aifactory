# Prospecting

## Purpose

Build and prioritize prospect lists from an explicit ICP using evidence-backed fit and timing signals.

## Workflow

1. Define firmographic, technographic, geographic and role criteria.
2. Define positive/negative buying signals.
3. Source candidates and preserve provenance.
4. Score fit separately from intent/timing.
5. Resolve decision-maker hypotheses and confidence.
6. Produce ranked account/contact list with why-now notes and data gaps.

## Guardrails

Never invent contact details, titles or intent signals. Respect privacy and outreach law.
