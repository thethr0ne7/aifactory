# Source Recovery

## Purpose

Recover missing, moved or weakly cited source material without silently substituting a different authority.

## Activate when

A cited URL is dead, content moved, a document version is missing, or an evidence chain references a source that cannot currently be retrieved.

## Workflow

1. Identify the exact missing source: owner, title, date/version, URL/identifier.
2. Search the source owner's current site/register first.
3. Search official mirrors, repositories or archives for the exact version.
4. Compare recovered copies for title/date/content consistency.
5. Preserve the recovered source and locator.
6. Mark provenance as recovered and note any uncertainty about version identity.
7. If exact recovery fails, keep the original claim `UNVERIFIED` rather than substituting a merely similar source.

## Guardrails

- Similar documents do not prove the original claim.
- Archive copies establish historical content, not current legal/operational validity.
- Never fabricate a missing citation.
