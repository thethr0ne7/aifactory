# Algorithmic Art

## Purpose

Create generative visual systems—patterns, particles, procedural backgrounds and data-driven art—that support a brand or product concept.

## Activate when

The brief explicitly benefits from generative or procedural visuals rather than static illustration.

## Workflow

1. Define the visual concept and relationship to Brand DNA.
2. Choose primitives, rules and controlled randomness.
3. Expose a small set of meaningful parameters.
4. Keep deterministic seeds when reproducibility matters.
5. Test composition across target aspect ratios and densities.
6. Reduce visual noise until hierarchy remains clear.
7. Validate CPU/GPU cost for interactive implementations.

## Guardrails

- Randomness must be controlled and purposeful.
- Decorative output must not reduce text/UI legibility.
- Generative backgrounds must not become a substitute for a coherent design system.
- Prefer deterministic reproduction for approved brand assets.
