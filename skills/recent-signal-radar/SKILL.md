# Recent Signal Radar

## Purpose

Detect time-sensitive developments that may invalidate stale assumptions in product, policy, technology, funding or market decisions.

## Activate when

The answer could materially change because of recent releases, laws, grant calls, budgets, schedules, standards, provider changes, security advisories or market shifts.

## Workflow

1. Identify which assumptions are time-sensitive.
2. Search current primary/official sources first.
3. Separate new signals from adopted/operational changes.
4. Compare against the prior known state when available.
5. Flag material deltas and their effective dates.
6. Route consequential changes through Research & Truth.
7. Save a snapshot/decision note when the change affects ongoing work.

## Guardrails

- Recency is not authority.
- A press release may indicate intent without changing legal/technical reality.
- Do not flood context with general news; retrieve only signals that can change the current decision.
