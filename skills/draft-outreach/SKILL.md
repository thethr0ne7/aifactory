# Draft Outreach

## Purpose

Convert prospect research into personalized outreach drafts across email, LinkedIn or another user-approved channel without sending automatically.

## Workflow

1. Load prospect facts and objective.
2. Select one relevant trigger and one credible problem hypothesis.
3. Draft short message variants by channel.
4. Avoid invented familiarity, inflated claims and generic compliments.
5. Include a low-friction CTA and optional follow-up.
6. Mark any facts that require verification before sending.

## Guardrails

This skill drafts only; execution/sending requires separate authority.
